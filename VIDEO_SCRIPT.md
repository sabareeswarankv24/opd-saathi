# OPD Saathi — 2-Minute Demo Video Recording Script

*Record your screen (using Loom, OBS, or Chrome Screen Recorder) with this script.*

---

### [0:00 - 0:20] Hook & Persona Introduction
*(Screen: Show the Home screen at mobile viewport 360px)*
> **Voiceover**: "In government hospitals across India, millions of elderly and low-literacy patients wait hours in chaotic lines just to get an OPD slip, only to wander lost trying to find their assigned room.
> This is OPD Saathi. Designed for Kamla, a 62-year-old grandmother who speaks Hindi, walks slowly, and avoids stairs."

---

### [0:20 - 0:45] Multilingual & Assisted Registration
*(Action: Click 'हिंदी'. Click 'पंजीकरण'. Toggle 'Someone is helping me / कोई मेरी मदद कर रहा है'. Type 'Kamla', 62, Female. Skip phone number. Show Emergency warning signs at the bottom).*
> **Voiceover**: "On first launch, patients choose their language in their own script — Hindi, Tamil, or English.
> Registration asks one simple question at a time. With 'Helper Mode', text expands, and narration is read aloud.
> Instead of confusing medical department names, Kamla picks from plain-language symptom cards like 'fever, cough, BP' for General Medicine."

---

### [0:45 - 1:10] Digital OPD Slip & Live Queue Tracking
*(Action: Submit to view the generated OPD slip with Token MED-045 and QR code. Click 'My Turn / मेरी बारी'. Show split screen with Staff Dashboard `/staff` calling next).*
> **Voiceover**: "Instantly, an OPD slip is generated with a token and QR code. 
> Kamla can see her exact position in line and estimated wait time based on doctor speed. 
> When she is 3 patients away, her phone buzzes with audio and a simulated SMS alert telling her to start walking to Room 1 on the first floor."

---

### [1:10 - 1:35] Dijkstra Step-Free Navigation & Landmark Directions
*(Action: Click 'Directions / रास्ता दिखाएँ' or go to 'Find a Place'. Ensure 'Avoid Stairs' is ON. Show the route from Main Entrance to Room 1).*
> **Voiceover**: "Finding the room is effortless. Using Dijkstra's shortest-path algorithm, OPD Saathi maps the exact step-by-step route on interactive floor plans. 
> With 'Avoid Stairs' enabled, it strictly routes Kamla through ramps and elevators, using recognizable visual landmarks like 'opposite the blue chairs' or 'beside drinking water'. 
> And if she feels lost, a single tap displays an 'I'm Lost' staff-assistance card in English and Hindi."

---

### [1:35 - 1:55] Emergency Priority & Staff TV Display
*(Action: Tap 'I need urgent help'. Switch to the Staff Dashboard to show the red alarm banner flashing. Switch to `/board` to show the tri-lingual TV display).*
> **Voiceover**: "Crucially, technology never delays urgent care. The Emergency and 'I need urgent help' buttons are pinned across every screen with zero login or token required. A single tap sounds an immediate alarm on the Staff Dashboard. 
> Meanwhile, the hospital waiting hall TV board cycles live tokens and voice announcements across English, Hindi, and Tamil."

---

### [1:55 - 2:05] Closing
*(Screen: Back to OPD Saathi Home)*
> **Voiceover**: "OPD Saathi transforms a stressful hospital ordeal into a dignified, guided care experience for every citizen. Thank you."
